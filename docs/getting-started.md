# Getting started

A walkthrough from an empty timeline to a small history. It takes about ten minutes. If you'd rather look around a
finished one first, run **Evra Timelines: Open the sample timeline** from the command palette: it opens *The
Chronicle of Veld*, a small world with eras, spans, lifespans and linked notes.

In this manual, **Settings → …** means the timeline's own settings panel: the sliders button at the bottom right of a
timeline.

## 1. Make a timeline

Click the Evra button in the ribbon, or run **Evra Timelines: New timeline** from the command palette. You can also
right-click a folder and choose **New timeline**.

A timeline is a file in your vault ending in `.evra`. It opens as a line of time running top to bottom, with a year
ruler along the edge.

## 2. Add some events

Double-click beside the line. A card appears on the nearest year, on the side you clicked. Type a title, press
**Enter**, then type a description. Pressing **Enter** in the description saves the card and starts the next one, so
you can write several in a row. Press **Esc** when you're done.

## 3. Move them

Drag a card along the line to change its date; it snaps to the marks on the ruler. Hold **Alt** to move by single
days. Drag a card across the line to put it on the other side.

**Ctrl/⌘ + scroll** (or pinch) zooms. **F** fits everything on screen. Every change can be undone with **Ctrl/⌘ Z**.

## 4. Make a span

Some things take time: a war, a reign, a voyage. Drag a card's dot along the line and the moment becomes a span, drawn
as a thread that leaves the line at its start and rejoins it at its end. Or select a card and press **S**.

## 5. Mark an era

Drag along the line itself to select a stretch of time, then choose **New era**. Give it a name and a color. Drag
again inside it and the menu offers **New sub-era of …**: eras nest to any depth.

## 6. Make the calendar yours

Open **Settings → Calendar**. Start from a preset, or name your own months and give each its length. Add leap days,
festival days between months, and weekdays if your world has them. Cards keep their named dates when the calendar
changes. [Calendars](calendars.md) has all of it.

## 7. Link a card to a note

Drag a note from the file explorer onto the timeline. It becomes a card that shows the note's opening lines, and
follows the note if you rename or move it. A card you already have can be linked from its **⋯** menu, with **Link to
note…**, or turned into a new note with **Convert to note**.
