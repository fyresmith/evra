# Settings

Evra has two places for settings. Almost everything belongs to a timeline and is saved in its file; a few options
belong to the plugin.

## The plugin's settings

In Obsidian, **Settings → Evra Timelines**.

### Files

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Folder for new timelines | Empty | Where a new timeline goes. Empty: the same place as new notes |
| Folder for notes made from cards | Empty | Where **Convert to note** puts a card's new note. Empty: next to the timeline |
| Open linked notes | Beside the timeline | Where a card's note opens: beside the timeline, in a new tab, or in place of the timeline. Hold Ctrl or ⌘ while clicking to open it in a new tab instead |

### New timelines

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Starting settings | Says what new timelines start with. **Forget saved settings** goes back to the built-in 12-month calendar |
| Sample timeline | **Open the sample** makes *The Chronicle of Veld* in its own folder and opens it |

To choose what new timelines start with, set a timeline up the way you like, then use **Use these settings for new
timelines** in that timeline's **Settings → Timeline → Defaults**. It saves the calendar, its formats, the colors and
the card options.

## A timeline's settings

The sliders button at the bottom right of a timeline opens its settings panel. Changes apply at once, are saved in the
timeline's file, and can be undone with **Ctrl/⌘ Z**. Each tab also has a command, such as **Settings: calendar**.

| Tab | What's in it | More |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar | A preset to start from, months, leap days, weekdays, unit names, how years are numbered, a second calendar, importing from Calendarium or Fantasy-Calendar | [Calendars](calendars.md) |
| Formats | The template behind every date label, with its tokens and a live preview | [Date formats](formats.md) |
| Timeline | The timeline's name, direction, range of years, what dragging snaps to, the "now" marker, how eras are shown, export, defaults for new timelines | [User guide](user-guide.md) |
| Cards | Card width, spans as threads or blocks, tinting cards with their color, when a crowded year collapses into a group card | [User guide](user-guide.md#spans) |
| Colors | The color presets cards and eras use | [User guide](user-guide.md#events) |
| Notes | Note sync, which properties it writes and what they're called, creating cards from dated notes, removing the properties again | [Linked notes and note sync](notes.md) |
